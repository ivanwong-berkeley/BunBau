1. Executive Summary

Milestone v6.7 resolves critical architectural, usability, and data partition concerns raised during production audits of the Tax Expert workspace. This release delivers a synchronized 4-card strategy system matching top portfolio metrics, an interactive line-item card inspector with continuous multi-year progression strips, rigorous multi-tenant data partitioning eliminating synthetic demo identities from live filings, and contextual safety scoping for deletion operations across portfolio and tax domains.

2. Key Remediations & Architectural Enhancements

2.1 Symmetrical 4-Card Tax Strategy System & Action Targeting

Prior to v6.7, the top metrics displayed Capital Loss Shield ($3,000), Suspended Passive Rental Loss ($44,500), and Marginal Drag (44.3%), but only 2 recommendations were provided below. The guidance for Suspended Passive Loss was entirely missing.

1. Options Neutralization (Emerald)
Cash-settled options collars and box spreads to offset short-term gains against ordinary brackets.
2. Suspended Passive Loss (Purple)
Form 8582 carryover monetization via Passive Income Generators (PIGs), IRC §469(g) qualifying complete dispositions, and MAGI phase-out calibration.
3. Muni Bond vs CD Drag (Amber)
Double-exempt California municipal bond yield arbitrage (+3.18% spread) avoiding Medicare IRMAA cliffs.
4. Safe Harbor Prepayment (Blue)
110% prior-year liability safe harbor compliance and W-4 Line 4(c) catch-up withholding under IRC §6513(b).

Clicking "Take Action" in TaxAnomalyBanner now dispatches target card IDs, switches subtabs, auto-scrolls into view via scrollIntoView, and pulses a target action highlight badge.

2.2 Interactive Line Item Card Inspector

In LongitudinalComparisonCard.tsx, users can now click any row or the sticky [Inspect] button to open the LineItemCardModal. The card features:

  • IRS Form & Line Reference: Form 1040 Line 1z, Schedule E Line 26 / Form 8582, Schedule D Line 21, CA 540 Line 48.
  • 9-Year Progression Strip: Chronological historical values with compound annual growth rate (CAGR).
  • Tax Statutory Impact: Clear explanations of phase-outs, deductions, and rate brackets.
  • 1-Click Deep Links: Jump directly to the exact page and line in BunBauDocumentViewer without compromising the virtualized continuous scroll viewer (*"Don't mess it up"*).

2.3 Multi-Tenant Partitioning & Identity Isolation

Real tax returns uploaded by users now cleanly replace synthetic demo names with their authenticated identity (user.displayName or filename metadata parsed by extractTaxpayerNameFromFilename). Synthetic identities (*"Alex & Jordan Taylor"*) are strictly quarantined to Demo Mode.

2.4 Contextual Danger Zone & Upload Modal Delete Safety

The trash icon in UploadValidationModal.tsx is now context-aware:

  • In tax context (context === 'tax' || activeTab === 'pdf'), the trash icon triggers TaxDeleteModal with typed phrase confirmation ("Delete all tax history" or "Delete <Year> tax history").
  • Brokerage portfolio deletion is strictly shielded, preventing any accidental wiping of investment accounts.
