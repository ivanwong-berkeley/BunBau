3. Component Remediation Specifications

3.1 Symmetrical 4-Card Strategy System & Deep "Take Action" Linking

Card 2: Suspended Passive Rental Loss Offset & Monetization Strategy (Form 8582)

Matches top purple metric ($44,500 Suspended Loss from 742 Evergreen Terrace). Provides actionable advice on: (1) Passive Income Generators (PIGs) to absorb losses 100% tax-free; (2) IRC §469(g) Qualifying Disposition to unlock full $44,500 against ordinary income upon complete sale; (3) Modified AGI Management to restore the $25,000 active rental allowance under $150k phase-out.

Card 4: Federal Safe Harbor Prepayment & W-2 Withholding Calibration

Matches blue Safe Harbor anomaly ($51,062 remaining). Details the 110% prior-year rule (IRC §6654(d)(1)(C)) and W-4 Line 4(c) catch-up extra withholding to retroactively cure early-quarter 1040-ES shortfalls and eliminate IRS Form 2210 penalties.

3.2 Interactive Line Item Card Inspector in Multi-Year Audit

In LongitudinalComparisonCard.tsx, clicking any line item row or the dedicated sticky [Inspect Card] icon on the line item column opens the Line Item Card Inspector:

  • Form & Page Reference: Displays IRS Form, Line number, and Form Page (e.g. Form 1040 Line 11, Page 1).
  • Multi-Year Progression Strip: Full historical matrix with annual values, YoY changes, and CAGR.
  • Tax Insight: Explains statutory rules, MAGI interactions, IRMAA thresholds, and NIIT impacts.
  • 1-Click PDF Deep-Link: Direct jump button into BunBauDocumentViewer without touching or disrupting the full viewer.

3.3 Clean Multi-Tenant Data Partitioning & Identity Isolation

Eliminates all instances of "Alex Taylor" and "Jordan Taylor" from Live Mode. The application derives real taxpayer identities from user.displayName and uploaded return metadata. Synthetic demo identities are strictly confined to Demo Mode with an explicit Tax Demo Mode banner.

3.4 Contextual Danger Zone & Tax Deletion

In UploadValidationModal.tsx, when context === 'tax' or activeTab === 'pdf', the trash button opens the granular tax deletion flow (TaxDeleteModal.tsx) requiring typed confirmations ("Delete all tax history", "Delete 2024 tax history", "Delete from 2020 to 2025 history"). The brokerage portfolio is 100% safeguarded from accidental deletion.
