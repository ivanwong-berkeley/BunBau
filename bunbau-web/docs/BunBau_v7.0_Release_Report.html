1. Executive Summary

Milestone v7.0 delivers a major precision upgrade to BunBau's multi-year tax analytics, executive KPI presentation, and PDF document workstation. Addressing production audits and user feedback across 5 UI screenshots, v7.0 resolves:

  • Overview KPI Year Focus & Chronological Processing: All tax filings are sorted and processed chronologically (2016..2025 or ..2026). Overview KPI cards explicitly spotlight the latest filing year (2025 Adjusted Gross Income, 2025 Total Tax, 2025 Effective Tax Rate, 2026 Loss Shields) with Latest Filing (2025) badges and clear vs 2024 comparison trend deltas.
  • React Portal-Based Detach Workstation: Detaching the PDF viewer now uses createPortal(..., document.body) with z-[100]. It spans 100% of the display viewport (100vw × 100vh), eliminates left menu overlap, grants the TOC sidebar full breathing room, and includes a uniform workstation header with Attach / Dock, Open in New Window, and Close controls.
  • Streamlined PDF Player Toolbar & Uncrushed Keyword Search Box: Replaced the horizontal 9-button multi-year explosion with an elegant, compact Tax Year Dropdown ([ 2025 Return ▾ ]), freeing up over 350px of toolbar width. The keyword search bar now has guaranteed flex bounds (min-w-[200px] max-w-sm flex-1) and an instant clear button (X).

2. Key Architectural Deliverables

1. Chronological Dynamic Tax KPI Engine (2025 Focus)

In TaxContext.tsx, active tax returns are sorted chronologically ascending (sortedTaxReturns). The comparison engine dynamically determines latestTaxReturn (2025) and previousTaxReturn (2024). Cards in TaxExecutiveSummaryCards.tsx prominently display the latest filing year in the title and badge, while comparisons explicitly compare against the prior year (vs 2024).

2. React Portal-Based Workstation Detach (Zero Left Menu Overlap)

The detached PDF viewer in BunBauDocumentViewer.tsx mounts directly onto document.body via React Portal at z-[100], completely outside the application's inner stacking context. The BunBau left navigation menu (z-20) stays beneath it, eliminating overlap and giving the Table of Contents (TOC) 100% visibility.

3. Streamlined PDF Player Toolbar & Uncrushed Keyword Search Box

Toolbar replaced the wide row of 9 multi-year buttons with a compact <select data-testid="viewer-year-select"> dropdown. The search container has dedicated flex bounds (min-w-[200px] max-w-sm) flanked by shrink-0 button clusters, ensuring the search box is never crushed.
