Executive Summary

Milestone v6.5 comprehensively addresses four critical user requirements for enterprise tax return operations: allowing unlimited multi-year PDF file ingestion with incremental historical retention; implementing typed safety confirmation phrases for tax history deletion (strictly isolated from financial accounts); re-architecting the PDF document viewer with continuous vertical scrolling and page virtualization capable of rendering 50–100 pages per file at 60fps with pinpoint line targeting; and scaling the analytics and comparison engine to an N-Year Longitudinal Comparison Matrix (up to 10 years) with sub-50ms execution.

Detailed Implementation Pillars

Pillar 1 Unlimited Multi-Year PDF Ingestion & Incremental History

• Universal PDF Uptake: Enhance UploadValidationModal.tsx and TaxMultiUploaderModal.tsx to accept .pdf and application/pdf, with an intelligent document classifier separating tax returns from brokerage statements.
• Multi-File Batch Queue: Ingest 5 to 10 years of tax return PDFs simultaneously with no limit on file count.
• Incremental History: Persists each year under users/{uid}/taxReturns/{year}. When 2026 is uploaded next year, it appends to existing 2020–2025 history without requiring re-uploading.
• Financial Data Safety: Existing CSV and screenshot ingestion pipelines for brokerage accounts remain 100% untouched and protected.

Pillar 2 Granular Tax History Deletion with Typed Safety Confirmation

Enforce strict safety confirmation phrases in the History and Danger Zone managers:

• Delete All History: "Delete all tax history" • Delete Single Year: "Delete <Year> tax history" (e.g., "Delete 2024 tax history") • Delete Multi-Year Range: "Delete from <FromYear> to <ToYear> history" (e.g., "Delete from 2020 to 2023 history")

Durable purge clears records from both Firestore and local storage cache, automatically returning the user to Tax Demo Mode if all returns are removed.

Pillar 3 High-Performance Continuous Scroll Virtualized PDF Viewer

• Continuous Vertical Scroll Canvas: Replaces static single-page card with a full multi-page continuous document stream.
• Page Virtualization: Renders active pages dynamically to maintain silky smooth 60fps scrolling across 50–100 pages per file.
• Accurate Line & Page Targeting: Clicking an audit bookmark (e.g. Schedule B, Schedule E, Form 8582, CA 540) smoothly scrolls to the exact page offset and pulses the target line with an emerald highlight.
• Manual Scroll Fallback: User can freely scroll up/down from any anchor point to inspect neighboring pages manually.
• Multi-Year Switcher: Instantly switch between 5–10 years of ingested PDFs directly from the viewer header.

Pillar 4 10-Year Longitudinal Scalability Engine

• Multi-Year Comparison Engine: Scales from 2-year diff to an N-year longitudinal matrix (up to 10 years, e.g., 2016–2025).
• Macro Trends & Sparklines: Tracks AGI, Taxable Income, Federal/State Tax, and Combined Effective Rate % across the entire decade.
• Loss Shield Cumulative Ledger: Tracks year-over-year creation, utilization, and carryforwards of capital losses and passive rental losses.
• Active Year Grounded Strategy: Keeps forward-looking life planning (Social Security 62 clawback, Medicare coordination, IRMAA cliff, SECURE 2.0 RMD age 75) laser-focused on the active tax year while drawing context from the 10-year trajectory.
• Performance: Sub-50ms execution via memoization.
